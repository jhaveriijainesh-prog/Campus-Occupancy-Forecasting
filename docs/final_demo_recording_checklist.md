# Final Demo Recording Checklist

## BEFORE RECORDING
- Close unrelated applications and browser tabs.
- Ensure no personal or private information is visible in the desktop or browser.
- Confirm no API keys, secrets, or environment values are visible on-screen.
- Start Docker Desktop and confirm the local stack is running.
- Check the API health endpoint and the readiness endpoint.
- Confirm the dashboard is reachable at http://localhost:8501.
- Maximize the browser window and remove unnecessary clutter.
- Disable notifications and other pop-ups.
- Verify the microphone is connected and working.
- Test microphone volume before the first take.
- Verify the screen recording resolution is appropriate for the final MP4 export.
- Choose a quiet room or reduce background noise.

## DURING RECORDING
- Follow the exact demo sequence in the final script.
- Do not click randomly or open unrelated tabs.
- Pause briefly after important results, especially after the forecast result.
- Keep cursor movement controlled and professional.
- Avoid exposing credentials or raw internal errors.
- If an accidental error occurs, stop and restart that segment rather than improvising.
- Keep the narrative focused on the verified local workflow.
- Keep the full recording within the 5–8 minute target.

## AFTER RECORDING
- Watch the full recording once without skipping.
- Check audio quality and microphone clarity.
- Check screen readability and browser focus.
- Make sure no secrets or credentials are visible.
- Confirm the forecast result is visible and legible.
- Confirm the final conclusion is included and the explanation stays within scope.
- Check the total duration stays within the target range.
- Export the final MP4 in standard quality for sharing or submission.

## RECORDING SAFETY RULES
- Do not claim a live video was created if it was not actually recorded.
- Do not fabricate an MP4 or presentation video.
- Keep the final script and final recording aligned with the verified report and evidence.
- Stay honest about synthetic data and local runtime boundaries.
